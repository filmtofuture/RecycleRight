Plastic Type

Not sure if your plastic film can be recycled? Try the Triple-S Test.

Triple-S Test
Is it...
Test if your plastic film is eligible for recycling in this bin.
1

Stretchable?

The plastic should be elastic and should not tear when pulled.

2

Soft?

The plastic should be easily folded and not sound squeaky when crinkled.

3

Silky?

The plastic should look dull under the light; it should not be very shiny or glossy.

If the answer to all three questions is...YES
Your plastic film is eligible to be recycled in this bin!Ensure your plastic film is clean and dry before putting it in the bin.

If it is not eligible, please dispose of it in a regular trash can.

Types of Plastic

Resin Identification Codes are embossed symbols on manufactured plastic products. They look like a triangle with a number inside. The number corresponds to the type of plastic the product is made of.
